Executive Leadership & Future Innovation

Meet the visionary leaders guiding TTT CARE toward a future where compassionate healthcare and advanced Artificial Intelligence work together to improve lives, support families, and create healthier communities worldwide.

Chairman & Strategic Advisor

Tisan Faroque

As Chairman & Strategic Advisor, he provides long-term strategic direction, corporate governance, and visionary leadership for TTT CARE. His mission is to build a trusted healthcare organization that combines compassion, innovation, and modern technology to serve humanity.

Future Vision

AI-Powered Healthcare Assistant

TTT CARE is committed to shaping the future of healthcare by developing intelligent AI-assisted technologies that support both patients and caregivers.

Our future healthcare assistant is envisioned to provide medication reminders, sleep support, health monitoring, wellness guidance, emergency alerts, and compassionate daily assistance for elderly individuals, patients, and families.

By integrating Artificial Intelligence with human-centered care, TTT CARE aims to make healthcare more accessible, reliable, and supportive for communities around the world.
